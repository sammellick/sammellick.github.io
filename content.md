## Adiunkt @ [Jagiellonian University](https://im.uj.edu.pl/en_GB/start-en)

Measured dynamics, probability, ergodic theory, and all that jazz. I work with [Mikolaj Fraczyk](https://sites.google.com/view/mikolaj-fraczyk/). If your problem statement involves the words 'invariant random', give me a call.

Previously postdoc @ McGill University working with [Anush Tserunyan](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/atserunyan/index.html) and [Marcin Sabok](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/msabok/), and postdoc with [Damien Gaboriau](http://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/) @ École Normale Supérieure de Lyon.

Former PhD student of [Miklós Abért](https://users.renyi.hu/~abert/) at Central European University.

Email: samuel.mellick[in French they call it *arobase*]uj.edu.pl

5. **Poisson-Voronoi tessellations and fixed price in higher rank**, joint with [Mikolaj Fraczyk](https://sites.google.com/view/mikolaj-fraczyk/) and [Amanda Wilkens](https://www.math.cmu.edu/~awilkens/). 7. **Point processes, cost, and the growth of rank in locally compact groups**, joint with [Miklós Abért](https://users.renyi.hu/~abert/). See [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07710) (*Israel Journal of Mathematics*).
8. **The Palm groupoid of a point process and factor graphs on amenable and Property (T) groups**, solo dolo. See [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07238) (*to appear* in a forthcoming survey on the connection between connected Cayley factor graphs and measure equivalence)
